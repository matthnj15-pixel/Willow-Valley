Assigned stock / example capacity


Storage acceptance and proposed trade policy are separate choices. Turning acceptance off would prevent future deliveries; it does not delete held goods. A policy cannot guarantee a merchant, supply, price, funds, space or instant freight.


All proposed policies default to Keep. No trade executed.


Keep holds stock for local use. Import buys toward a reserve target when possible. Export sells only above a protected reserve target. Balance imports below a minimum and exports above a higher maximum. Prices, staffing and capacity units await gameplay design; current limited manual caravan trades remain unchanged.
